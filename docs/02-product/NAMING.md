# Naming

## Current working name: Facet
One person, several sides: work, personal, each client. Every account is a facet with its own colour. Short, easy to say in English and Arabic, says nothing about Anthropic's marks.

## Rejected
| Name | Why |
|---|---|
| Switchyard | First working name. Fine legally, but describes switching, while the research says the product is control and costs. |
| Lanes | Taken: lanes.sh, a local-first desktop app for AI coding agents. |
| Tessera | Taken: horang-labs/tessera, a workspace for AI coding sessions. |
| Anything with "Claude" | Anthropic's terms forbid it in product names, logos and domains (Clawdbot was asked to rename in January 2026). |

## Before launch
- [ ] Trademark search in the US, EU and the seller's country (class 9 software, class 42 SaaS).
- [ ] Domain and GitHub org availability.
- [ ] Check app stores (Microsoft Store, Mac App Store) and Flathub/Snap for conflicts.

No Claude logo, no starburst-like mark, no "works with Claude" badge.
