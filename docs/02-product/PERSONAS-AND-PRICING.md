# Personas and pricing

## Personas

### Nadia, freelance developer (paying core)
Three client accounts plus her own Max plan. Bills hourly and wants to pass AI costs through. Today she copies numbers from `/usage` into a spreadsheet and sometimes types client A's code into client B's account.
**Needs:** colour-coded accounts she cannot confuse, per-client cost reports, budget alerts, invoice export.
**Pays for:** Pro.

### Omar, employee with a work seat and a personal plan (the way in)
Team seat from his employer, personal Pro at home, both on one Linux laptop. Signs out and in several times a day and has lost Code-tab history doing it.
**Needs:** both accounts open at once, a clear sign of which is which, his employer's policy respected.
**Pays for:** probably nothing at first. Brings word of mouth and GitHub stars.

### Lina, agency tech lead (future Team buyer)
Eight developers, each with client accounts. Wants consistent settings, an approved list of MCP servers, and to know nobody installed a risky plugin.
**Needs:** policy packs, approved catalogue, audit trail.
**Pays for:** Team, later.

### Not a persona
People stacking Max plans to stretch limits. Free tools already serve them and building for them invites enforcement.

## Pricing (proposal, validate with interviews and a waitlist)
| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Free (open source) | $0 | Unlimited accounts on Linux, Windows, macOS; colours and tint; guided sign-in; environment scrubbing; basic settings editor; Timeline and costs for one account at a time; folder rules; sessions; health |
| Pro | $9/month or $89/year | Cross-account Timeline and costs; drill to tool call; client budgets and alerts; invoice export; settings presets and copy between accounts; ecosystem risk scores; weekly report |
| Team (later) | ~$15–20/seat/month | Shared policy packs, approved catalogue, audit, sync |

Why subscription, not one-time: keeping up with Claude releases on three systems is ongoing work. At a 3% conversion, 10,000 free users give 300 buyers: about $2.4K a month at $8 versus a one-off $8.7K at $29.

Payment notes: merchant-of-record fees (5% + $0.50) favour annual plans. Lemon Squeezy pays out to many MENA countries but not all; check the seller's country. Windows code signing for individuals outside the USA and Canada: use the Microsoft Store.
