---
description: Record a product or architecture decision in the decision log
---

Add a new entry at the top of the log in `docs/04-plan/DECISIONS.md` for: $ARGUMENTS

Use the next number and this shape:

```
## D-NNN: <short title>
Date: <YYYY-MM-DD> · Status: accepted | proposed | superseded by D-NNN
Context: what forced the decision, with links to research or spike results.
Decision: what we will do.
Consequences: what this makes easier, harder, or rules out.
```

If it supersedes an older decision, update the old entry's status too.
